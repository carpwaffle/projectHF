Hack FPS · Map blueprint · v3 proposal

Clock Tower District시계탑 지구 · a city map with named landmarks, three lanes and real sniper lanes

A replacement for the current Downtown map. Every building is a named landmark you can call out ("Hospital roof", "Crane", "Subway"), the map runs on three lanes with an underground and a rooftop layer, and there are four sniper perches built as duels, each with its own counter-perch. Blue spawns in the north, Red in the south, and the layout is mirrored across the middle so neither team has an advantage.

Plan

North is up. Blue (north) and Red (south) halves are mirror images, so each building has a twin of the same shape and height. The twins look different and have different names (Hospital ↔ Hotel, Bank ↔ Police Station), so a callout always means exactly one place. The centre row has one-of-a-kind landmarks that both teams fight over.

Sniping

This answers the second piece of tester feedback. Every perch is built as a duel. Each one can be seen from at least one other perch and has at least two ways up, so a sniper is never safe for long. No sightline reaches a spawn exit. The longest lines are 70–77 m. Today's map is 97 m across, and buildings cut most lines well short of that.

S1Hospital roof → down the Canal (and Hotel roof → up it)~76 m15 mThe enemy's own roof is the counter-sniper. Flank by the roof's inside stairs or its outside fire escape

S3Clock Tower balcony → both halves of Main Street~38 m14 mSeen from both roofs, the crane, and Bank / Police windows. One stairway up, so attackers can trap you. Strong but risky

  • No sightline into a spawn. Each base's exits face sideways into its back avenue, behind an arcade, so a new player isn't shot from across the map.
  • Lane widths set the weapon. Canal and rooftops for the sniper (50–77 m). Main Street and the Plaza for rifles (20–40 m). Subway, Market and the Warehouse / Factory for P90 and shotgun (under 15 m).
  • Snipers are needed in fights. Every high perch overlooks somewhere players have to cross: the Canal Bridge, the Plaza, or the market entrances. Snipers have targets without being able to lock down a whole lane.

Verticality

There are three height layers, and there are ways between them in every lane. That way, height is something you climb to during a fight, not a separate area. Every high point has at least two ways up (Overwatch's rule for high ground). Some extra routes open only with hacks, which makes hacks worth buying.

Below street level

  • Subway (−4.5 m): one E–W tunnel from the Canal to Construction, under the Plaza. Four ways in: the Subway building, 2 Plaza stairways, and Construction.
  • Canal (−2.5 m): 4 ramps out. Players in the canal bed are hard to hit from street level but easy targets from the roofs.

Above the street

  • Skybridges (3F) link the Hospital and Bank (Hotel and Police Station in the south) over Main Street. Players walk across, and the bridges also hide each base from the Clock Tower.
  • Warehouse / Factory catwalks run around a 9 m hall above the containers: two levels in one room, like Nuke.
  • Construction is open-sided: you can be shot from the Plaza while climbing the scaffolding.

Hack routes

  • Speed hack (3.1 m jump): jump straight out of the canal without the ramps, onto market stall roofs and up to Market Hall 2F windows, and onto Construction 2F without the scaffolding.
  • Fly hack: reaches the Clock Tower top and the crane cab without the ladder, at the cost of hanging in open air.
  • No-clip: the Subway's floor and walls stay solid, so no-clip can't drop you anywhere hidden.

Callouts in the game

Named buildings help, but the game should also show the names. This is cheap to build.

  • Location under the minimap: e.g. "병원 · 4F" or "Hospital · 4F". Zone outlines are exported from Blender to map_meta.json, and the game checks which outline the player stands in. This adds no draw calls.
  • Teammates' locations on the scoreboard (Tab) and as faint labels on the minimap, which the map hack already shows.
  • Kill feed with the place: "carp → bot3 (Crane)". The host already sends feed keys, so this adds one field.
  • Rooftop signs with an icon and the name in Korean and English. They all come from one texture, so the signs cost one draw call.
  • A different outline for each building (station arch, city hall dome, factory chimney, crane, clock tower), so you can tell where you are from the skyline alone.

Checks to pass when it's built

  • Walk every route on both halves: spawn → each lane → enemy spawn; every stair, ramp, ladder and skybridge.
  • Cast rays from each sniper perch: no clear line to any spawn exit; S1–S5 lengths match the table (±5 m).
  • Time from spawn to Plaza is 7–9 s for both teams; the two halves give matching times.
  • Speed hack jumps work at every route listed above, and normal players can't make them.
  • Bots can still get everywhere: the bot route planner needs new connection points for ladders and the Subway.
  • Frame time on a mid-range laptop: compare 60 fps at the Plaza, on the Hospital roof and in the Subway with today's map.

Open questions

  • Symmetry: mirrored halves (this plan: both teams have the Canal on their west) or rotated halves like today's map (each team has the Canal on a different side)? Mirroring keeps lane names the same for everyone.
  • Size: 96 × 128 m suits 4v4 to 6v6. For smaller rooms the Market Alley and Warehouse / Factory could be closed off as a "small" version.
  • Ladders: the game has no ladders yet. The crane and tower top need either ladders (new movement code) or tight switchback stairs.
  • Build order: start with plain grey boxes for the whole layout and play-test lanes and sightlines, then add textures building by building.
